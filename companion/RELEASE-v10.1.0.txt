Istanbul · Viking Travel Companion v10.1.0 — Traveler Invitations & Roles

- Adds email-based invitations for the active trip.
- Adds Owner, Editor and Viewer roles.
- Adds pending, accepted and revoked invitation states.
- Lets owners change roles and remove travelers.
- Lets invited travelers accept after signing in with the invited email.
- Keeps decrypted private Vault content separate from shared trip membership.
- Adds Firebase email/password account creation.
