Disembarkation at Fusina

Viking docks at Marghera on August 31, then repositions to Fusina for September 1 disembarkation. From Fusina, continue with luggage to Hotel al Sotoportego in Cannaregio.

Begin the Fusina → Venice transfer.
For four adults with cruise luggage, the simplest independent option is a prearranged private transfer that combines a road vehicle from the Fusina terminal with a water taxi to the closest practical landing for Hotel al Sotoportego. First follow any Viking-provided disembarkation transfer instructions; do not assume the public Fusina ferry serves the cruise terminal.
Hotel handoff: ask the transfer provider for the closest practical landing to Hotel al Sotoportego, 5595/A, Cannaregio. The hotel entrance is discreet, so keep the street number and map pin visible for the final walk.
