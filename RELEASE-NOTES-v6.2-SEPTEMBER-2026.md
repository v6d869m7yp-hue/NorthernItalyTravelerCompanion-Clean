# Northern Italy Traveler Companion v6.3 — Hero & Image System

## Scope
This release standardizes the visual treatment of photographic and illustrated page openers without restructuring destination content.

## Changes
- Introduced one responsive hero language for homepage, illustrated destinations, and hotel pages.
- Restricted dark overlays to safe text zones so photographs remain bright and detailed.
- Preserved full Dolomites and Cinque Terre illustrated opener pages without cropping printed titles.
- Added safe title widths and natural word wrapping at desktop, iPad, and phone breakpoints.
- Standardized `contain` behavior for editorial spreads and `cover` behavior for photography.
- Removed filters from printed guide spreads and gently lifted photographic brightness for outdoor viewing.
- Promoted the Dolomites and Cinque Terre opener images to high-priority loading.
- Updated manifest, service-worker cache, footers, and asset query strings to v6.3.

## Deferred
Navigation and destination/hotel template restructuring remain scheduled for later staged releases.
